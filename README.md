# BRT-IN-MORELIA
# ASSESSMENT OF THE OPERATIONAL PERFORMANCE AND ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT OF A PROPOSED BRT CORRIDOR IN MORELIA

This repository contains the supporting materials for the manuscript:

**This project is based on an assessment of the operational performance and environmental impact of the Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system, with the aim of evaluating its performance and understanding its contribution to mobility and environmental sustainability in the city of Morelia.

## Overview
This research evaluates the technical, operational, and environmental feasibility of implementing a Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system in Morelia, Michoacán. The study analyzes the existing public transportation system, traffic conditions, and the proposed BRT corridor to assess potential improvements in urban mobility and travel times. Using the Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO) software, current and projected scenarios will be modeled to quantify operational performance and pollutant emissions. The findings will provide valuable technical information to support decision-making and promote more sustainable urban transportation in Morelia.

## Contents
- `data/`: Traffic volume counts, vehicle classification data, and other input datasets.
- `videos/`: Traffic monitoring videos recorded with cameras for vehicle counting and validation.
- `sumo/`: SUMO simulation files, including the baseline scenario and four public transportation migration scenarios.
- `calibration/`:Calibration documents, parameters, validation data, and methodological records used to calibrate the SUMO model.
- `gis/`: GIS layers, spatial data, and files used to characterize and analyze the proposed BRT corridor.
- `results/`: Simulation outputs, traffic analysis results, emission estimates, tables, and figures generated from the study.
- `figures/`: Maps, graphs, and visualizations generated for the analysis and manuscript.
- `docs/`: Research documents, technical reports, methodology, references, and supporting documentation.
